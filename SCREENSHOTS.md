# System Screenshots, Captions and Descriptions

This section provides report-ready captions and descriptions for the screenshots of the **Famous Haven Hotel Management System**.

## Figure 1: Staff Login Page

**Caption:** Staff login interface for the Famous Haven Hotel Management System.

**Description:** This screen provides secure access to the administrative area of the system. Staff members enter their email address and password to log in to the dashboard. It also provides a link for customers who wish to create an account and reserve a room.

## Figure 2: Administrative Dashboard

**Caption:** Main dashboard displaying hotel operational summaries and performance charts.

**Description:** The dashboard gives staff an at-a-glance view of key hotel information, including occupancy rate, daily revenue, active bookings, and total guests. It also presents revenue-trend and weekly-occupancy charts to support quick monitoring and decision-making.

## Figure 3: Booking Management Page

**Caption:** Booking management interface showing reservation records and booking statuses.

**Description:** This page enables staff to manage reservations and check-ins. It displays pending, confirmed, and checked-in booking totals, and provides search and status-filtering tools. Each booking record includes the guest, room, check-in and check-out dates, payment status, amount, and receipt action.

## Figure 4: Create New Booking Dialog

**Caption:** Booking form for registering a reservation for an existing or new guest.

**Description:** The booking dialog allows staff to create a reservation by selecting either a new guest or an existing guest. The form captures the room, stay dates, and payment status before the booking is confirmed.

## Figure 5: Booked Rooms Page

**Caption:** Booked-room overview linking rooms to their customers and reservation details.

**Description:** This screen groups reservations by room and shows the number of booked rooms, total bookings, and customers. When a room is selected, staff can view the associated customer's contact details, booking dates, confirmation status, payment status, and total amount.

## Figure 6: Room Management Page

**Caption:** Room inventory page showing room availability, type, capacity, price, and maintenance status.

**Description:** The room management module enables staff to monitor and manage hotel rooms. It summarizes available, occupied, reserved, and maintenance rooms, while individual room cards show the room type, nightly rate, guest capacity, floor location, and controls for changing availability or maintenance status.

## Figure 7: Guest Management Page

**Caption:** Guest profile page displaying customer contact information and booking history summaries.

**Description:** This page presents guest records in profile cards. Each card displays the guest's name, email address, phone number, number of stays, amount spent, and registration date. Staff can search existing guests or add a new guest record.

## Figure 8: Register New Guest Dialog

**Caption:** Form for adding a new guest profile to the hotel management system.

**Description:** The guest-registration dialog captures the full name, email address, and optional phone number of a customer. Saving the form creates a guest profile that can be used for future reservations and customer management.

## Figure 9: Analytics Page

**Caption:** Hotel analytics dashboard showing revenue, bookings, occupancy, customer spending, and revenue by room type.

**Description:** The analytics module summarizes business performance through key indicators such as total revenue, confirmed bookings, occupancy rate, and total guests. It includes a bar chart of top customers by spending and a pie chart that shows how revenue is distributed across room types.

## Figure 10: Register New Staff Page

**Caption:** Staff-account registration form for creating hotel employee access.

**Description:** This page allows an authorized user to create an account for a new staff member. It collects the employee's full name, email address, temporary password, and assigned role, such as receptionist. The role assignment supports controlled access to system functions.

## Figure 11: Staff Management Page

**Caption:** Staff management interface listing employee roles and account statuses.

**Description:** The staff management page provides an overview of personnel with system access. It displays each staff member's name, role, active status, and available actions. Administrators can also add a new staff member or remove an existing account from this screen.

## Figure 12: Customer Room Reservation Page

**Caption:** Customer-facing room reservation form with booking summary and available-room list.

**Description:** This page allows customers to create an account and reserve a room. The form collects personal details, account credentials, room selection, stay dates, and card-payment details. The booking summary updates with the selected room, rate, payment status, and total, while the available-room panel helps the customer compare room options.

## Figure 13: Customer Reservation Dashboard

**Caption:** Customer portal displaying the guest's reservation history and upcoming stay details.

**Description:** After signing in, customers can view their reservations in the portal. Each reservation shows the assigned room, confirmation status, check-in and check-out dates, and total booking cost. The **Book Again** option provides a convenient path to make another reservation.
